NCEA Moderation Assistant

A second reader for internally assessed work. It advises; you decide.

Before you paste student work into any AI.
  • The Ministry and NZQA position is that AI may support marking but must not replace your professional judgement, and the Ministry discourages relying on it for summative internal marking. Treat every grade this produces as a second opinion to test against your own and your department's.
  • Use your school's approved AI account (for example Gemini signed in with your school Google account), not a personal free account that may train on what you upload.
  • Never include NSNs, and consider initials or codes instead of full names. Step 3 lets you choose.

1 · PasteOpen your school AI account, start a new chat, paste the prompt. The links above copy it for you.
2 · AttachAdd the schedule, task sheet and student work in the same message, then send.
3 · CheckIt confirms what it received and quotes your schedule. If anything is missing it stops and asks.
4 · VerifyWork through the Points to note and the suggested check-mark list before recording any grade.

Marking a whole class over several weeks? Save the prompt as a Gemini Gem or a Claude Project and put the schedule in its knowledge files, then only the student work needs attaching each time.
